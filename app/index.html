FAQ

Can anyone contribute?

Yes, with a bank account you’re authorized to inspect. Each bank takes one to five contributors, and one paid transcript per person.

Do I need to share my bank data?

Not publicly. Your agent verifies Peer Link’s enclave, then encrypts a read-only session and transcript to it. The enclave reads your transaction history and keeps only the redacted structure, without cookies, names, account numbers, balances or amounts. Nothing goes in Git, issues or pull requests.

How do rewards work?

Each bank’s issue sets a fixed $5 or $10 in USDC per accepted transcript, paid once to your wallet. Rewards shown here are planned. The issue says whether that bank is open and how many slots remain. See the campaign index.

Who pays for the AI?

You do. The check runs on your own API key, even if the transcript is rejected. Choose attested confidential inference or an approved provider such as OpenAI or OpenRouter, which will see the banking content it needs. Your agent shows the provider, limits and reward and waits for your consent.

Can I contribute today?

Only when your bank’s issue says it is open. Banks open one at a time as the submission service is verified. Until then nothing is collected or paid.

Does a transcript prove payment?

No. A transcript shows how a bank presents payments so Peer can build that bank’s integration. A listing does not mean an integration is implemented or approved for Peer payments.

What is Peer Link?

Peer Link is Peer’s open bank-integration library. It is not a bank and is unaffiliated with Plaid or the named financial institutions.
